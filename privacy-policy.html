Privacy Policy – Medicine Guide

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Medicine Guide ("the App") is an educational pharmacology reference app by Abdullah Khan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App uses and how.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account or sign-in, and we do not collect, store or sell your name, email, phone number or any other personal information ourselves.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and app-usage information to show and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where permitted by law. We do not receive or control this data. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Local storage on your device

The App stores simple preferences on your device only, such as your last page, bookmarks, theme, font size and whether you accepted the disclaimer. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else.

4. Saving pages to your Gallery

If you tap the camera button, the App saves an image of the current page to your device Gallery (Pictures/A-Z Pharmacology). The image stays on your device and is not uploaded by the App.

5. Medical disclaimer

The content of the App is for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before taking, stopping or changing any medicine.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
